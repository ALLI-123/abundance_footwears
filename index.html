Returns & Exchanges

×

7-Day Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If you're not satisfied, you can return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Tags and labels must be intact
  • Proof of purchase required
  • Sale items are final sale (non-returnable)

How to Return

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or email within 7 days
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. We'll provide return shipping instructions
  4. Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund or exchange

Refunds

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Want a different size or color? We'll happily exchange it subject to availability.

Size Guide

×

How to Measure Your Foot

  1. Place a piece of paper on the floor against a wall
  2. Stand on the paper with your heel against the wall
  3. Mark the longest point of your foot
  4. Measure the distance from the wall to the mark in centimeters
  5. Use the chart below to find your size

Frequently Asked Questions

×

How do I place an order?

You can order directly through our website by clicking "Buy Now" (opens WhatsApp), or contact us via WhatsApp/phone to place your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, cash on delivery (Lagos only), and mobile money. Payment details are provided after you place your order.

How long does delivery take?

Lagos: 1-2 days. Other states: 3-5 days. Rural areas: 5-7 days.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order is shipped, we'll send you a tracking number via WhatsApp or email.

What if the shoes don't fit?

No problem! We offer 7-day returns and exchanges. Contact us and we'll guide you through the process.

Are your products original?

Yes, all our products are 100% authentic and come with a quality guarantee.

Do you offer wholesale prices?

Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp for wholesale inquiries and special bulk pricing.

How do I care for my shoes?

Use our shoe care accessories (polish, brushes) to maintain quality. Store in a cool, dry place. Check our accessories section for care products.
